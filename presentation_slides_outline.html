AEGIS — 10-Slide Capstone Phase 1 Presentation Deck

Recommended slide-by-slide structure for Capstone Evaluation
Slide 1
Title Slide
  • Project Title: AEGIS — AI Enabled Emergency Response & Disaster Management Platform
  • Domain: Artificial Intelligence & Emergency Management Systems
  • Key Highlights: 6 AI Engines, Live Weather REST API, 2D/3D GIS Command Map, NDRF CAP Export
Slide 2
Problem Statement & Motivation
  • High response latencies during major Indian disasters (Hurricanes, Earthquakes, Floods).
  • Lack of automated triage, hazard spread prediction, and optimal evacuation route computation.
  • Incompatibility between local emergency logs and standardized national (NDRF / CAP) alerts.
Slide 3
System Architecture Overview
  • Single-Page Application (SPA) architecture built with Vanilla JS, HTML5 & CSS3.
  • Real-time telemetry integration with Open-Meteo Weather API.
  • PWA Service Worker offline caching for responder mobile smartphones.
Slide 4
The 6 Core AI Algorithmic Modules
  • Bayesian Network: Posterior threat likelihood P(Disaster|Telemetry).
  • A* Router: Evacuation route optimization with dynamic hazard cost weighting.
  • K-Means++ Clustering: Haversine spatial staging hub centroids.
  • Decision Tree Classifier: START protocol severity triage (L1 - L5).
  • Monte Carlo Simulator: 500-run stochastic cellular disaster spread prediction.
  • Neural Network Predictor: Feed-forward non-linear casualty & damage model.
Slide 5
GIS Command Center & 3D Perspective
  • Interactive Leaflet.js 2D GIS map displaying live incidents, fleet locations & danger zones.
  • High-performance 3D Tactical Perspective mode with elevated marker drop-shadows.
  • 5 regional scenarios (Chennai Hurricane, Uttarakhand Earthquake, Nilgiris Wildfire, Assam Flood, Jamnagar Chemical Spill).
Slide 6
Real-Time Operational Workflow
  • 6-step lifecycle: Detection ➔ AI Triage ➔ Spread Risk ➔ Route Optimization ➔ NDRF CAP Dispatch ➔ 3D Map Tracking.
Slide 7
NDRF CAP v1.2 Standard Alert Integration
  • OASIS Common Alerting Protocol compliant JSON/XML alert payload generation.
  • One-click export for National Disaster Response Force battalion servers.
Slide 8
Experimental Results & Performance
  • 60 FPS GIS map rendering & instantaneous AI inference (< 50ms execution time).
  • Zero framework bundle overhead for low-spec emergency command hardware.
Slide 9
Phase 2 Development Roadmap
  • YOLOv8 Aerial Computer Vision for drone damage analytics.
  • Whisper NLP Speech-to-Text for 112 emergency calls.
  • NDMA Sachet live server synchronization.
  • Offline Bluetooth LE / Wi-Fi Direct mesh networking.
Slide 10
Conclusion & Q&A
  • Summary of Phase 1 deliverables and platform readiness.
  • Open for Evaluator Questions & System Demonstration.
