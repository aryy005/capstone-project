2. End-to-End Operational Lifecycle (6 Steps)

Step 1
Disaster Event Detection & Ingestion

Emergency events enter the AEGIS Command Center through two continuous pipelines:

  • Live Environmental Telemetry: Automatic REST polling from Open-Meteo & IMD weather APIs for wind speed, rainfall, temperature, and humidity.
  • Citizen & Field Ingestion: Emergency calls, mobile responder reports, and satellite coordinates logging exact GPS location, disaster type, and population density.
Step 2
AI Automated Triage & Severity Scoring

Every incoming report is classified instantaneously by the Decision Tree START Triage Model:

  • Level 1 - Level 2 (Minimal / Low): Assigned to local municipal responders.
  • Level 3 (Moderate): Standard district-level emergency response.
  • Level 4 - Level 5 (High / Critical): Triggers automated multi-agency alerts, NDRF mobilization, and mandatory evacuation advisories.
Step 3
Real-Time Threat & Risk Simulation

Before dispatching rescue teams, dual AI engines evaluate secondary hazards:

  • Bayesian Network: Computes posterior probabilities for secondary hazards (e.g., coastal flooding triggering structural collapses or landslide risk).
  • Monte Carlo Simulator: Runs 500 stochastic iterations across a 1,600-cell grid to project disaster spread over the next 6 to 24 hours.
Step 4
Resource Clustering & Safe Evacuation Routing

The platform optimizes ground logistics:

  • K-Means++ Clustering: Calculates optimal geographical centroids for setting up Relief Camps & Staging Hubs across active incident clusters.
  • A* Pathfinding Router: Dynamically calculates clear evacuation corridors for rescue trucks while avoiding active danger zones.
Step 5
Emergency Dispatch & Team Alerting

Rescue teams are alerted via standardized protocols:

  • OASIS CAP v1.2 Format: Standardized Common Alerting Protocol JSON/XML payloads formatted for National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) servers.
  • PWA Mobile Push Alerts: Real-time dispatch alerts delivered to field responder smartphones with route navigation.
Step 6
Live 2D/3D Tactical Monitoring & Resolution

Command operators track rescue units (ambulances, fire engines, helicopters) live on the 2D / 3D Tactical Perspective GIS Command Map. Field teams update status progression: ACTIVE ➔ DEPLOYED ➔ CONTAINED ➔ RESOLVED.
