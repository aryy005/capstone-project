AEGIS — AI Enabled Emergency Response & Disaster Management Platform

Final Technical Architecture, AI Algorithms & Implementation Report

1. Executive Summary & Abstract

Natural disasters cause devastating human and economic losses due to delayed emergency response coordination. AEGIS is an end-to-end, AI-powered emergency management platform designed to automate disaster triage, predict hazard spread, calculate safe evacuation corridors, optimize rescue staging hubs, and broadcast standardized alerts to response agencies.

3. The 6 Integrated AI Algorithmic Modules

1. Bayesian Threat Inference

Calculates posterior disaster probabilities using recursive Bayes theorem P(Disaster|Evidence) based on live environmental sensor telemetry.

2. A* Pathfinding Evacuation Router

Computes optimal dynamic evacuation corridors using cost function f(n) = g(n) + h(n) with penalty weight W(e) = Dist × (1 + 1000 × Risk).

3. K-Means++ Resource Clustering

Calculates spatial centroids using spherical Haversine distance to establish optimal staging hubs and relief camp locations.

4. Decision Tree START Severity Triage

Classifies incoming emergency incidents into 5 severity levels (L1 Minimal to L5 Critical) based on magnitude and population affected.

5. Monte Carlo Stochastic Risk Simulator

Executes 500 stochastic simulation iterations across a 1,600-cell grid to project disaster spread probabilities over 24-hour windows.

6. Neural Network Damage Predictor

2-layer feed-forward neural network with Sigmoid activation layers predicting casualty estimates and monetary loss.
